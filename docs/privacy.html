Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 11, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how AnimeFights, operated through the GeneradorContenido platform ("we", "our", "the Service"), collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our automated content creation and publishing service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account configuration: Bot names, content niches, visual style preferences, and publishing schedules.
  • Contact information: Email address (optional, for notifications).

2.2 Information from Third-Party Services

When you connect your social media accounts (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram), we receive:

  • OAuth tokens: Access tokens and refresh tokens to publish content on your behalf.
  • User identifiers: Platform-specific user IDs (e.g., TikTok open_id).
  • Basic profile information: Display name and avatar (as permitted by the platform).

We only request the minimum permissions necessary to publish content on your behalf.

2.3 Automatically Generated Data

  • Generated content: AI-generated video scripts, video prompts, platform descriptions, and video files.
  • Pipeline logs: Records of content generation steps, publishing status, and error messages.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information exclusively to:

  • Generate and publish content to your connected social media accounts upon your approval.
  • Authenticate with third-party platforms on your behalf.
  • Maintain and improve the content generation pipeline.
  • Provide the approval workflow for reviewing content before publishing.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • All data is stored locally on the server where the application runs.
  • Sensitive credentials (API keys, OAuth tokens) are encrypted at rest using industry-standard Fernet symmetric encryption.
  • We do not transmit your data to any third parties beyond the connected social media platforms and AI service providers necessary for content generation and publishing.

5. Third-Party Services

The Service integrates with the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • TikTok API: For publishing videos to TikTok. See TikTok's Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube API: For publishing videos to YouTube. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Instagram API: For publishing videos to Instagram. See Instagram's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Gemini API: For AI-powered content generation. See Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Data Retention

  • Generated content and logs are retained until you explicitly delete them through the dashboard.
  • OAuth tokens are stored as long as your social media accounts remain connected.
  • You can delete all data associated with a bot by deleting the bot from the dashboard.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: View all data stored about your bots and content through the dashboard.
  • Delete: Remove any bot, content, credentials, or generated data at any time.
  • Disconnect: Revoke social media account connections at any time.
  • Review: Approve or reject all AI-generated content before it is published.
  • Data portability: Export your data through the dashboard interface.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

10. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, to exercise your data rights, or to report a privacy concern, please contact us through the application dashboard.
